- Self-update: while an update waits for its health check, a boot job starts the update watchdog after a reboot (#516): a systemd unit on Linux, a LaunchDaemon on macOS, a scheduled task on Windows. Before, the watchdog didn't survive a reboot, so an update that crashed before its health check started after a reboot was never rolled back. The boot job runs the watchdog copy of the previous release, never the new binary, and is removed once the update is confirmed healthy or rolled back. After a reboot the new binary gets a full 10 minutes to start; if it hasn't by then, the update is rolled back.
